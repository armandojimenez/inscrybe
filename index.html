Listening

How do I bring my subliminals into Inscrybe?

In the Library, tap + and choose Import Files, Import Folder or Import from a Link. You can also open an audio file in Inscrybe from Files, AirDrop or the Share sheet. A link has to point to the file itself; streaming services aren’t supported.

How do I repeat a track a set number of times?

In the player, tap Repeat, then under Repeat This Track choose a number of times or Custom…. With Then Play Next on, the next track plays after the last time; otherwise playback stops there.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the moon in the player and choose a length in minutes, End of Track, End of Folder or End of Playlist, depending on what’s playing. The sound fades out at the end.

Does the Apple Watch app play audio?

No. It’s a remote for your iPhone: it shows what’s playing, controls it and the sleep timer, and opens your Library and playlists. Your iPhone plays the audio.

The Studio

What does How Hidden measure?

How Hidden compares each voice in a project with the music and sounds over it and tells you where it sits: Clear, Murmur, Faint, Hidden or Buried. Tap a stop and the voice’s volume is set to reach it. It’s an estimate, so Can You Hear It? lets you check with your own ears.

How do I save a project as a track?

In the project, tap Save to Library and choose the format. When you save it again after an edit, Inscrybe offers to replace that track, keeping its plays and its place in your Library.

Privacy

What does Discreet Mode change?

A disguised track shows a made-up name and cover in Inscrybe and outside it: on the Lock Screen, in CarPlay, in widgets, on Apple Watch, in Siri and in the names of files you share. Turn it on in Settings › Privacy › Discreet Mode, for Private Items or Everything. Show Real Names shows the real ones in Inscrybe until you leave the app.

I forgot my App Lock PIN.

On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? and get back in with your device’s passcode or your recovery code, whichever you chose when you set up App Lock.

What does Inscrybe collect?

Inscrybe has no analytics: nothing logs how you use the app. If it crashes, a report is sent without your track names, your scripts or anything about you. Turn off Share Crash Reports in Settings › Privacy to send none.

Your account

How do I use Inscrybe on all my devices?

Turn on Settings › iCloud Sync on each device signed in to the same Apple Account. Your Library, playlists, projects and recordings sync through your own iCloud, and audio downloads when it plays.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

In Inscrybe, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

In Inscrybe, open Settings and tap Manage Subscription, or go to the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles billing.
